\begin{Head} 
1. Introduction
\end{Head}

\begin{Head} 
2. Literature review
\end{Head}

Use References to describe the current state of research. (Enumerate References list according to order of appearing in the text)

\begin{Head} 
2. The aim of the article.
\end{Head}

Goal of your research (shortly).

\begin{Head}
3. Methods
\end{Head}

Here must be described information 
about how you did the study.
Among all, here must be described 
what exactly has been done, 
how long the research did take,
what materials or equipment were involved,
what theorems have been used,
what data collection instruments and procedures have been used,
what analysis methods have been used,
why this method (of research, solving the problem or analysis) instead of another has been used.


\begin{Head}
4. Results
\end{Head}

Here must be described research results without their analysis.
New definitions, theorems, obtained data and results of computational experiments. Just reporting, commentaries and 
  interpretation of the results are presented in Discussion section.

\begin{Head}
5. Discussion
\end{Head}

Here must be summarize the main findings of the study,
described connection between these findings and other research,
discussed flaws in the current study,
described possible future research,
stated the implications of your findings for future policy or practice.

\begin{Head}
6. Conclusion
\end{Head}
Aim and main results in single short form.

\begin{Head}
7. Aknowledgements (optional)
\end{Head}
 Name people who contributed to the work, but did not contribute sufficiently to earn authorship. 
 You must have permission from any individuals mentioned in the acknowledgements sections.
